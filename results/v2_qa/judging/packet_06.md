# Packet 06 (glyphs on sheet_06.png)

## q051

**A**
- shows: high definition, video quality, media enhancement, digital resolution, plus symbol, addition, premium feature, visual clarity, screen technology, display standard
- means: media editing; video production; quality assurance; technical upgrades; content optimization; visual design
- note: The icon is highly specific to media, video, and display technology. It is useful for tasks related to technical media production or hardware configuration, but too niche for general productivity tasks.

**B**
- shows: letters H, D, R inside a circle; modifiers: plus sign in bottom right quadrant
- means: HDR+ (High Dynamic Range Plus) camera or display mode; HDR video or photo format setting
- note: The icon consists only of the letters 'HDR+' inside a circle, representing a technical camera/display setting or property (High Dynamic Range Plus). Under the rules, icons showing only letters or technical settings/properties rather than a concrete task subject should be discarded.

## q052

**A**
- shows: download, save, import, install, archive, fetch, retrieve, transfer, inbox, receive
- means: downloading files; importing data; installing software; archiving documents; fetching remote resources; receiving incoming items
- note: This icon is a standard UI glyph for downloading or importing. While it is highly functional for specific technical tasks, it is too generic for general task management and lacks a strong semantic metaphor for non-digital activities.

**B**
- shows: hollow downward-pointing arrow over a horizontal bar; modifiers: horizontal base line beneath arrow tip
- means: download or save to device; install or fetch update package; export or extract data to local file; move to bottom or align bottom
- note: A downward arrow pointing to a horizontal bar is the universal glyph for downloading or saving files locally. In a task list, it fits action-centric tasks where downloading, exporting, or cleaning up the Downloads folder is the core work, though tasks about a specific object (like a book or map) often prefer that object's icon.

- note: The icon depicts three figures inside an enclosure with steam lines rising above, clearly representing a sauna, steam room, hot tub, or heated studio class. While somewhat niche, it is the exact visual match for spa outings, bathhouses, hot yoga, and home sauna or jacuzzi maintenance.

- note: The icon represents precision, focus, and targeting. It is useful for tasks involving calibration, goal setting, or narrowing down a specific objective, but it is too abstract for general administrative or routine tasks.
