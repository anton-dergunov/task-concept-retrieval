# Packet 33 (glyphs on sheet_33.png)

## q321

**A**
- shows: countertop cabinet with curved faucet and cup on top
- means: kitchen sink / countertop; doing the dishes / washing up; kitchen / kitchenette; bathroom vanity
- note: The icon depicts a two-door base cabinet topped by a counter, a curved gooseneck faucet, and a cup or bowl, clearly representing a kitchen sink or countertop area. It fits common household chores like washing dishes, cleaning counters, and under-sink plumbing repairs, though at tiny sizes the upper shapes can vaguely resemble the bow on a gift box.

**B**
- shows: gift, box, package, present, surprise, delivery, unboxing, opening, container, wrapped item
- means: receiving; giving; acquiring; unwrapping; opening; delivering
- note: The icon visually represents a gift or a package, suggesting tasks related to receiving, sending, or organizing physical items. It has a clear metaphor and can be consistently interpreted for tasks involving tangible goods.

## q322

**A**
- shows: prohibition, no, stop, forbidden, restricted, circle with slash, negation, ban, disabled, cancel
- means: prohibiting; canceling; disabling; blocking; rejecting; avoiding
- note: This icon is a universal UI glyph for 'prohibition' or 'no'. While it has a clear meaning, it is too negative and restrictive to be useful for general task categorization in a productivity app, as most tasks are constructive rather than prohibitive.

**B**
- shows: circle with diagonal slash from top-left to bottom-right; modifiers: diagonal slash inside circle
- means: prohibition, ban, or forbidden; block, deny, or restrict access; cancel, revoke, or opt out; quit or stop a habit
- note: The universal prohibition sign (circle with a diagonal slash) is instantly recognizable for banning, blocking, opting out, or cancelling abstract services and habits. However, when cancelling or avoiding a concrete object with its own visual representation (like a flight, dentist appointment, or coffee), the specific object's icon is a much better fit.

At small sizes it is easily confused with a power button, upload tray, or compass/navigation indicator, making it poor as a glanceable subject reminder.

- note: This is an OS status-bar indicator showing a specific Wi-Fi signal strength level (half full) rather than a general Wi-Fi symbol. Because it encodes a transient state/quantity and is redundant with a standard Wi-Fi icon, it is UI chrome and should be discarded.
