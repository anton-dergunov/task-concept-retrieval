## q391

**A**
- shows: camera, photography, video recording, lens, shutter, disabled, blocked, off, no recording, privacy
- means: disable camera; stop recording; privacy settings; restrict media access; camera maintenance; block video feed
- note: The icon clearly depicts a camera with a slash through it, representing a state of being disabled, blocked, or turned off. It is highly effective for tasks related to privacy, hardware toggles, or stopping media capture.

**B**
- shows: square frame containing a segmented circle or pie chart; modifiers: diagonal slash from top-left to bottom-right
- means: analytics off / disable tracking or telemetry; no chart / hide data visualization or statistics; offline / no web or network connection (if read as a globe in a frame)
- note: The icon depicts a framed pie chart or segmented circle crossed out with a diagonal slash, representing disabled charts, analytics, or telemetry. Because the slash clips the frame and circle heavily, it is hard to read at small sizes and is only useful for a narrow set of tasks around turning off tracking or removing charts.
