# Packet 42 (glyphs on sheet_42.png)

## q411

**A**
- shows: looping squiggly line with a solid circle above right; modifiers: solid circular dot at upper right
- means: touch gesture or stylus stroke; freehand drawing, scribble, or handwriting; signature or autograph; swimming or diving person (pareidolia)
- note: The icon depicts a looping freehand stroke with a touch point dot (standard UI glyph for touch/stylus gestures or scribbling). While useful for niche tasks around trackpad gestures, digital inking, handwriting practice, or signatures, it is abstract enough that many users confuse it with a person swimming, a Japanese character, or a piece of string.

**B**
- shows: scribble, doodle, abstract line, swirl, curve, dot, handwriting, sketch, random path, ink stroke
- means: brainstorming; doodling; note taking; sketching; creative exploration; drafting
- note: The icon is too abstract and lacks a clear, universally recognizable semantic meaning. It resembles a random scribble or doodle, making it difficult to associate with specific, actionable task categories in a professional productivity context.

## q413

- note: A five-pointed star outline is universally recognizable and maps cleanly to tasks about leaving ratings/reviews, managing favorites/starred items, stargazing, or star decorations. - note: The star is a universally recognized symbol for priority, importance, or favoriting. While it is somewhat generic, it is highly effective for distinguishing high-value or critical tasks in a long list.

## q420

**A**

- means: camera off / video disabled / audio-only call; broken webcam, camcorder, or security camera; no filming or recording allowed; cancelled video meeting
